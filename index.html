Winston Gmail Manager

A private, single-user tool. Not offered to the public.

Winston Gmail Manager is a personal application that helps its owner manage his own Gmail inbox. It reads, searches, labels, archives and drafts replies to messages in the owner's account, using an AI assistant (Anthropic's Claude) to summarize and classify mail.

Actions that cannot easily be undone, such as sending mail or deleting drafts and filters, always require the owner's explicit confirmation before they run.

The application is used only by its owner. It is not available for sign-up and does not access any other person's account.
